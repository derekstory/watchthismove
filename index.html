How we do business

Premium games. Modest prices.

We build games with the craft and depth of full-priced titles, then price them so cost is never the reason someone doesn't play. Good games should be within everyone's reach — that's the point of making them.

Pay once. Own it forever.

One price buys the whole game — and every course, mode, and update we ever add to it. No editions, no DLC, no season passes. When we make the game better, everyone who owns it gets the better game.

No ads. No subscriptions.

A game you've bought shouldn't keep charging you — with your money or your attention. Nothing we make will ever interrupt play to sell you something.

Try it before you buy it.

Every game we make ships with a free demo that never expires — a real slice of the real game, no time limit, no strings, yours to keep. In The 14th Club, that's the entire front nine at Herons Marsh. You should know a game is worth your money because you played it, not because an ad told you.

Your data stays yours.

We keep only the minimum that makes optional online play possible — an anonymous player ID, the display name you choose, and the scores you post. No account, no email, no tracking, nothing sold or shared. Your motion is read on your device and never leaves it, and everything online can be deleted from inside the game, any time.
