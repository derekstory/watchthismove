Privacy

Effective August 2026

The short version

This website collects nothing about you.

This site

watchthismove.com is a set of static pages. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics, embeds no trackers, and has no forms — there is nothing here that collects, stores, or shares information about visitors.

The site is served by GitHub Pages. As with any web host, GitHub's servers may log standard technical data (such as IP addresses) to deliver the pages and keep them secure; that logging is governed by the GitHub Privacy Statement. We never see it.

Our games

Each of our games carries its own privacy policy, published where the game lives. For The 14th Club, see the14thclub.com/privacy — the short version there: your swing stays on your phone, we don't know who you are, and there are no ads.

Contact

Questions about privacy or anything else: info@watchthismove.com. If our data practices ever change, this page will say so.
